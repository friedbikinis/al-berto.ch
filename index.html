Who are we?

Al-Berto & the Fried Bikinis is not a classic band project – we are a journey.

Most people believe that artists create songs. In our experience it is the other way around.

The Fried Bikinis never intended to become a band or beeing played on the radio or in movies. The truth is: the songs took control over this project and used it as a vessel to travel around the world.
